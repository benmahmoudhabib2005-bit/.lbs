Outfit tips

Quick, practical rules to help you dress better every day — no stylist needed.

01
Colour

The 3-colour rule

Limit your outfit to 3 colours max — one neutral, one base, one accent. Keeps any look cohesive without overthinking.

02
Fit

Fit beats price every time

An average item in a great fit always beats an expensive item that doesn't suit your body. Tailoring is the best fashion investment.

03
Layering

Master the 3-layer system

Base layer, mid layer, outer layer. Works for style and temperature control in every season.

04
Occasion

Dress one level up

When in doubt, dress slightly smarter than the occasion requires. It signals care and confidence.

05
Accessories

Accessories carry the look

A simple outfit with a quality watch, belt, bag, or shoes can look completely transformed. Invest in accessories first.

06
Capsule wardrobe

Build a core 10

10 versatile pieces that mix and match create 30+ outfits. Think neutral basics, quality jeans, a blazer.

07
Shoes

Shoes make or break it

Clean, well-maintained shoes elevate any outfit. Dirty or worn shoes undermine even the best look.
